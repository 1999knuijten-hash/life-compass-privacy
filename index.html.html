Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 October 2026

1. About Life Compass

Life Compass is a private, personal application used by its owner to combine and analyse health, sleep, recovery, activity, training, nutrition and life-context information for personal coaching and decision support.

2. WHOOP data

Life Compass accesses WHOOP data only after the user explicitly authorizes access through WHOOP's OAuth flow. Depending on the permissions granted, this may include recovery, sleep, cycle/strain and workout data.

Access can be revoked by the user. Life Compass requests only the permissions needed for its personal health and coaching functionality.

3. Storage and use

Data retrieved from WHOOP and data entered into Life Compass are primarily stored locally on the user's own computer. The information is used only for personal analysis, coaching, visualisation and related Life Compass functionality.

Life Compass does not sell personal data and does not use personal data for advertising.

4. AI processing

Selected information may be sent to third-party AI services, including the OpenAI API, when needed to generate personal analyses, summaries or coaching. Only information relevant to the requested analysis should be sent.

5. Data deletion and access revocation

The owner can stop using Life Compass, revoke WHOOP authorization and delete locally stored Life Compass data at any time.
